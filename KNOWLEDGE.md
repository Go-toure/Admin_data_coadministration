# What the admin data system knows

Generated by `python admin.py rules --markdown`. The rules live in `add_coadmin_columns.py`; this is their catalogue.


## Co-administration

- **coadmin**: Same district, round and Response with an nOPV2 record and a bOPV record are merged into one 'nOPV2 & bOPV' record; TotalNbrVaccPolio = the larger of the two (same children). A combined record gets the same total in both columns until the country gives the split.  
  Source: Feedback from Valentina Bigoni, WHO AFRO. Switch: `(always)`.
- **unused-vaccine**: A vaccine that was not used has an EMPTY total and coverage (not 0). Real reported zeros are kept.  
  Source: Decision 30 Oct 2026 (Ahmed's convention). Switch: `(always)`.

## Population

- **poppolio**: PopPolio is corrected from vaccinated / CVPolio when coverage is impossible (above 1.5, or below 0.5 with a plausible CVPolio); decimals rounded; otherwise the district median of the year; then a gradient-boosting estimate (listed for confirmation).  
  Source: Quality work with the focal points. Switch: `--no-fix-poppolio, --no-model-poppolio`.

## Copy / drag-fill errors

- **copy-repair**: A Response of another country, or nOPV3..nOPV15, is restored in this order: the LOOKUP (country + vaccine + start date), the newest earlier backup, the other rows of the same country/date/round, the campaign footprint. Only unique answers are used; the rest is UNRESOLVED.  
  Source: The lookup is the primary reference (your rule). Switch: `--no-repair, --no-infer`.
- **copied-rounds**: Rounds that the lookup does not have and that only copy another round of the same Response and date are removed and listed.  
  Source: Found in the 2026 data. Switch: `--keep-copied-rounds`.

## Key fields

- **response-fill**: An empty Response is filled from the lookup (exact start date, or within 21 days with the same round number), only when unique.  
  Source: Portal check 'Missing key field'. Switch: `--no-fill-response`.
- **response-names**: A Response that is not a lookup name but starts the name of the one lookup round of that country within 7 days is renamed to the lookup's spelling (MLW-2026-02-1_nOPV -> MLW-2026-02-1_nOPV-NID).  
  Source: Decision 30 Oct 2026: follow the lookup. Switch: `--no-align-response-names, --align-response-names COUNTRY`.

## Coverage

- **cvpolio**: An empty CVPolio is filled with TotalNbrVaccPolio / PopPolio. Records with only a target (no vaccinated) stay empty.  
  Source: Request of 28 Oct 2026. Switch: `--no-fill-cvpolio`.

## Districts

- **district-fill**: An empty District is filled from a recovered file, matched on country + Response + round + province: same PopPolio and total; same PopPolio and total within 10%; same total; total + the target implied by the coverage; the last unmatched pair; same PopPolio or total with unique pairing.  
  Source: Ethiopia file AFRO-ETH FIVE REGIONS R1 (282 of 282 filled). Switch: `--district-source FILE`.
- **addis**: Addis Ababa rows that repeat the sub-city name (NIFAS SILK LAFTO x13) are renamed '<SUB-CITY> WOREDA NN' from the file, only when every row of the sub-city matches a different woreda. Rows without a source (round 2 and 4 sub-cities of round 1) are left; they stay possible duplicates until a woreda-level file exists.  
  Source: Request of 29 Oct 2026. Switch: `(with --district-source)`.

## Geography

- **afrogdb-code**: Two columns, geo_code and geo_match, are added at the end of the sheet: the AFROGDB district code of the Country / Province / District, found with the AFRO-Geo-Reference rules (exact name, same name ignoring accents and punctuation, the one district of that name in another province, or a reviewed crosswalk decision; then the extended layers of crosswalk_extended.csv: manual decisions, Ethiopia woreda to zone, relaxed, old-shape overlay, name similarity; geo_match says which, and the last two are marked probable). The names themselves are NOT changed. A name that is not found has an empty code and is listed in review/afrogdb_unmatched.csv of the build (names and counts only). The check warns when the share of records with a code falls.  
  Source: Request of 16 Nov 2026 (same reference as LQAS, IM and SCOPE). Switch: `--no-afrogdb-codes, --no-afrogdb-extended, --afrogdb DIR`.
- **province**: A Province that belongs to another country (per geo_reference.csv) is corrected when certain.  
  Source: Admin portal checks. Switch: `--no-fix-province`.
- **country-names**: A Country spelled another way (CAMEROUN, REPUBLIQUE CENTRAFRIQUE, TANZANIA ...) gets the standard name of the geography reference (CAMEROON, CENTRAL AFRICAN REPUBLIC, UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA). Only names of a fixed list whose standard name is in the reference.  
  Source: Request of 4 Nov 2026. Switch: `--no-fix-country-names`.
- **province-spelling**: A Province that is the same name as an Admin1 of the geography reference written differently (spaces, accents, punctuation, filler words: MAI NDOMBE = MAINDOMBE, PROVINCIA DE NAMPULA = NAMPULA, WADIFIRA = WADI FIRA, RS 3 = RS3) gets the reference's spelling. Exact match only, and only when the district does not belong to another province of the reference. Near matches and new Admin1s (Cuando, Sidama ...) are never changed: they are listed in the fix list.  
  Source: Request of 4 Nov 2026. Switch: `--no-fix-province-spelling`.

## Population

- **digit-error**: A target population that is 10 or 100 times off (a missing or extra digit) is corrected only when the reported coverage (vaccinated / CVPolio) implies the corrected figure within 10% AND that figure is 0.6 - 1.7 times the median target of the same district's other campaigns. Both must agree; otherwise it stays flagged.  
  Source: Request of 4 Nov 2026. Switch: `--no-fix-digit-error`.

## Checks

- **fix-list**: What cannot be corrected safely (missing key fields, report dates, target above total population, coverage above the ceiling, very different targets, coverage that differs, provinces to confirm, combined and separate records, records entered twice) is written to Fix_list_by_country.xlsx: one sheet per problem, with the row, the column to correct, the current and the suggested value. Cut per country in the app.  
  Source: Request of 4 Nov 2026. Switch: `--no-fix-list`.

## Intake

- **submissions**: The files the countries send (SharePoint folder Admin_data_submissions, copied read-only to workspace/submissions_in by 'admin.py submissions pull', which 'submissions inspect' runs first) are inspected one by one. The decisions (accept / reject, who, the note) are made in the Operations Center and kept in its AdminOps folder; a build on the PC reads that ledger first (read only, ADMIN_STORE_SHARE_URL) so that it adds exactly what the app accepted, and says so in the manifest and in the check when it could not: layout (template, template with other header spellings, mapped by certain synonyms, or not usable), normalisation of every cell (capitals, spaces, country / province / vaccine / round spelling, dates, numbers as text, percentages, Year / SIAMTH / Ctry_Code / AFRO_block, the constants the live file has), the portal quality checks, the campaigns against the lookup, duplicates inside the file, and every record against the live file (new / same / other figures). A file with no campaign name (a Nigeria eTally or tally) is wired to the lookup by its date: a campaign keeps its planned start date, so data dated on or after a planned start belong to that campaign until the next planned start (at most 60 days back), data of a campaign that ran up to 21 days before its planned start belong to that campaign, and anything else stays unwired for a person; Response, round and the planned date are written and the date in the file is kept in the note. When the file gives a round the lookup does not have (Cycle_2 in the file name, a round column, a hint) and the campaign that started last before the date has its last lookup round just below it, within 60 days, the records are the next round of the same campaign: same Response, that round, the date of the records; the lookup is then missing a round, which is listed in the report sheet Lookup_gaps, and a person confirms. An operator accepts or rejects each file; only accepted files are used, and only their NEW records are added at the end of the next build, before every other rule. Records already in the live file are never changed by a submission: the differences are listed.  
  Source: Request of 5 Nov 2026. Switch: `admin.py submissions inspect | list | accept | reject; build --no-submissions`.
- **submissions-clean**: What the intake no longer asks a person about (16 Nov 2026). NO LOSS (17 Nov 2026, automatic rule 4): every new record that passed the validation and the standardisation and is not a duplicate goes into the live file, also when its file has other findings; a record that fails (an Error of the quality checks, the target or the number vaccinated is not a number (text in a calculated column does not hold a record), a key field still empty, another country than the folder) is held back with its reason in the sheet Held_back_records and in prepared/<hash>_held.xlsx, as are records that look like live ones with other figures or match several live records. Files in the wrong folder or with a vaccine that disagrees with the lookup wait whole. NAME ALIGNMENT (17 Nov 2026): a record at the same place, within 3 days of the same date and with the same vaccine as exactly one live record is that live record under another campaign name or round (CAM-2025-11-01 for CAM-2026-01, NOPV2 for bOPV-nOPV2, Rnd3 for Rnd1): it is not added, not held, and the pair is listed as an alias in the inspection. FEEDBACK TO THE COUNTRIES: `python admin.py submissions feedback` writes Feedback_to_countries.md and .xlsx (one line per country, file and matter: target population missing, failed check, empty fields, text instead of numbers, unit not clear, same place and date with other figures, campaign name or round differs, vaccine differs from the lookup, sheet not read, wrong folder, calculated columns with errors), from the held-back records and the findings, in plain words with what the country does; nobody has to write it by hand. NOT ADMIN DATA: a file with no admin columns whose name says IM, LQAS, AEFI, supplies, call-in or a summary is labelled NOT ADMIN DATA and ignored; a file with no template columns and no such name is NOT ignored (it is reported as another layout). (1) Coverage above the ceiling is for reference only: the live file has its own coverage columns, so the records are kept and the inspection only says how many there are. (2) Duplicates are removed: a record with the same place and the same figures as a live record under another campaign name, round or date is not added (the report counts it as removed); a copy of the same new records in two files with the same figures is kept once by the build. Only records with other figures than their twin are left for a person. (3) New records are standardised with the lookup: an empty or near-spelled Response gets the lookup name, an empty round gets the lookup round, and a named campaign whose records carry another date gets the lookup's PLANNED start date (the file's date goes in the notes); this uses the same date rules as the wiring of records without a campaign name, and the round the file gives is respected. Standardised dates are listed (code date_standardized) and the lookup-confirmed rule can accept them.  
  Source: Request of 16 Nov 2026. Switch: `none: always on in submissions inspect`.
- **submissions-auto**: Automatic rule 1 (switch: submissions_auto in admin_settings.json, off by default). A file nobody has decided on is taken into the build by itself only when every finding of its inspection is harmless: records already in the live file with other figures (never changed by a submission), a header mapping that was certain, records left out, many warnings. A file with an Error, a coverage above the ceiling, a date far from its campaign, a campaign guessed from the live file or the file name, values never seen, cells that are not numbers, a possible duplicate, records also new in another file or an eTally conversion always waits for a person. A decision of a person, accepted or rejected, always wins. Rule 3 (the engine learns from the people): a file with the same name pattern, country folder and layout as a file a person accepted is taken again when it shows no kind of finding that one did not show and none of the hard ones (an Error, several live units, a possible duplicate, an overlap, another country, cells that are not numbers, a vaccine that differs from the lookup, an unreadable sheet). admin.py submissions summary lists, by country, where the new records are, what holds them back and who can fix it, and which lookup rounds with no data are in a submitted file. Rule 2 (needs an inspection made by this version): when every campaign round of the file is a round of the lookup (same country, campaign, date and round) and the vaccine agrees, a campaign taken from the lookup or the file name, a year typed wrongly and an eTally export added up by LGA are harmless too. The files taken are listed in the log, in the manifest and in the check.  
  Source: Request of 16 Nov 2026. Switch: `--no-auto-submissions; admin.py submissions auto lists what the rule would take`.

## Hygiene

- **text-clean**: Stray control characters (carriage returns), line breaks and double / edge spaces in Country, Province, District, Response, Vaccine_type and Round_Add are removed.  
  Source: Found in District (KAJIADO EAST) 30 Oct 2026. Switch: `--no-clean-text`.
- **dates**: SIA_date written as text ('18-sept-20') becomes a real date.  
  Source: Admin portal. Switch: `(always)`.
- **duplicates**: Exact copies are removed. A record entered twice (same key AND the same population and results) is reported as Duplicate record and removed only with --resolve-duplicates. Records with the same key but other population or results are different units (most records have no sub-district): never removed, reported only as information (Same names, other figures). Key = Country, Province, District, [Admin_3], SIA_date, Round_Add, Vaccine_type, Response, compared without accents, case or punctuation.  
  Source: Admin portal. Switch: `--no-dedupe, --resolve-duplicates; admin.py duplicates lists them and what a fix would do; the Operations Center has the same choice on the Cycle tab`.

## Checks

- **quality**: The portal quality checks (missing key field, coverage above the ceiling, target jumps, report date, province/country, co-administration consistency, vaccine spelling, duplicates) produce a report with Errors / Warnings / Info.  
  Source: Admin portal (run_admin_quality_check.R). Switch: `--no-quality`.

## Decisions and standing rules

- The lookup file is the primary reference for campaign names, dates and rounds; backups come second.
- Never guess: an answer is used only when it is unique; otherwise the record is left and listed.
- A build always starts from the live SharePoint file, so nobody's newer edits are lost.
- SharePoint keeps no version history (the local backup has it); one published file, replaced in place.
- The data dictionary (AFRO_admin_data_dictionary.xlsx) is pushed with a publish only when its content changed (compared cell by cell); then it replaces the existing SharePoint file, and the previous live one is kept in workspace/dictionary_backups. A dictionary problem never undoes the published data (exit 3, retry with `admin.py dictionary`).
- The Operations Center app keeps only the newest 3 backups of the live file on SharePoint (the PC keeps all of them); the dictionary is pushed only when it changed.
- Data is never committed to git (only lookup.xlsx and geo_reference.csv). No attribution lines in commit messages.
- Each code change is its own commit, followed by its own push.
- Open questions kept visible: Addis Ababa round 2 and 4 sub-cities lack woreda names (need a woreda-level file); Tegedie exists in two zones of Amhara with the same name; about 1,460 Nigeria 2022-Jan 2023 records have no Response in the lookup.
